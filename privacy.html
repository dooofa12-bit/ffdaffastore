Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Minimal Data Collection

FFdaffastore operates on a strict need-to-know basis. This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics trackers, or commercial advertising scripts.

2. Transaction & Payment Privacy

Payment receipts and buyer verification data sent via direct transfer or third-party escrow (such as Itemku) are kept strictly confidential. We never store bank passwords, PINs, or sensitive payment credentials.

3. Direct Chat Communication

Chat logs conducted via WhatsApp, Discord, or Instagram are preserved solely for the duration of the 3-month warranty period to process any potential warranty claims, and are never disclosed, rented, or sold to third parties.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding data handling or wish to remove your chat history following the expiration of your 3-month warranty, please reach out via our official contact channels.
